### 8. Scroll-sip: girl (red) / guy (blue) taking a sip on scroll
True "person drinks as you scroll" needs **video or a frame sequence**, and consistent AI-generated human video is the costly, hard-to-nail part.
- **Full version (expensive):** a 2–4 s looping clip per side (girl sipping red under red neon; guy sipping blue under blue neon), scrubbed to scroll. Tools: Kling / Runway / Veo / Hailuo.
  > Prompt (girl): *Cinematic 3-second clip of a beautiful young woman in a red-and-magenta neon-lit setting slowly raising a red drink bottle and taking a sip, subtle head tilt, glossy skin, sunset-neon rim light, shallow depth of field, premium beverage ad, seamless loop.* (Guy version: swap to a young man, royal-blue/cyan neon, blue bottle.)
- **Cheaper alternative I recommend first:** one striking still portrait per side + a subtle scroll parallax/zoom (no video). 80% of the effect, ~5% of the cost/risk.

*Rec: start with the still-portrait + parallax alternative. Only commission the video if the page really needs motion there.*
